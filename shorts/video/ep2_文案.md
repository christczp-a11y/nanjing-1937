## 3. 简介（英文，直接粘贴）

```
9 December 1937: Japanese planes drop a letter on Nanjing. Surrender by noon tomorrow. Nobody answers.

At 1 pm on the 10th, about 70,000 Japanese troops attack a Ming-dynasty wall. At Guanghua Gate engineers blow a breach; two companies get in and can't get out — one loses 80 of its 88 men. On Purple Mountain the attackers are told not to shell the side with Sun Yat-sen's tomb, so they take it ridge by ridge. In the south, two divisions need two days to take Yuhuatai hill.

On the 12th the wall breaks. At 5 pm General Tang Shengzhi orders a breakout. 12,500 men try it in the dark; 3,000–4,000 get through. Everyone else runs for Yijiang Gate, the only way to the river. It is half blocked, and its guards were never told of the retreat. They open fire. Tang crosses that night by steamboat. One division reaches the water with 3,000 men; 300 survive.

Japan counted 1,558 of its own dead for the battle. For the Chinese army there is no agreed number.

Part 2 of 3. Part 1: how Nanjing became a trap. Part 3 tomorrow.

Numbers are secondary sources cross-checked; where historians disagree we show a range.
Map data: Natural Earth (public domain); elevation from AWS Terrain Tiles.
```

## 8. 注意

- 「570-year-old」：城墙始建于 1366 年，到 1937 年约 570 年。
- 1,558 是日方自己统计的阵亡数（另有负伤 4,619），不含山田支队，评论区有人问可以这样答。
- 「四万人」是两个师团的总兵力，旁白没说全部投入，简介写的是「two divisions」。
