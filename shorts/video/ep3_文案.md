## 3. 简介（英文，直接粘贴）

```
13 December 1937: the battle for Nanjing is over. For the people still inside, the worst six weeks are about to begin.

The day before, Japanese planes sank the American gunboat USS Panay 28 miles upriver. That made the front pages. News of what was happening inside the city took three more days to get out.

Chinese soldiers who could not escape threw away their uniforms. The Japanese army searched for them by checking men's hands. At one place on the riverbank the army itself counted 14,777 prisoners, machine-gunned over three nights. About twenty foreigners set up a Safety Zone in the west of the city; some 250,000 people crowded inside. Its chairman, John Rabe, was a member of the Nazi Party. It was safer. It was not safe: the Tokyo tribunal found about 20,000 rapes in the first month. The army gave its 70,000 soldiers in the city 17 military police. On 17 December the commander, Matsui Iwane, rode in for his victory parade. From the 19th the army burned about a third of the city.

Burial societies counted more than 155,000 bodies. The Tokyo tribunal (1948) found that more than 200,000 people were murdered; the Nanjing tribunal (1947) found more than 300,000. Matsui was hanged in 1948. The wall is still there.

Part 3 of 3. Full film next week.

Sources: International Military Tribunal for the Far East judgment (1948); Nanjing War Crimes Tribunal (1947); Lewis Smythe, War Damage in the Nanking Area (1938). Japanese historians' estimates range from about 40,000 to about 200,000.
Map data: Natural Earth (public domain); elevation from AWS Terrain Tiles. Historical photos: Wikimedia Commons.

```

**简介：**
```
1937 年 12 月 13 日，南京的仗打完了。对城里的人来说，最黑暗的六个星期才开始。
前一天，日机在上游 28 英里处炸沉美国炮舰帕奈号，这条新闻上了头版；城里发生的事，又过了三天才传出去。
日军搜捕脱下军装的中国士兵，看手上有没有老茧。江边一处，日军自己点过的俘虏有 14,777 人，三个夜里被机枪射杀。二十来个外国人在城西划出安全区，约二十五万人进去避难，主持人拉贝是纳粹党员。那里安全一些，但并不安全：东京审判认定头一个月发生约两万起强奸。城里七万日军，只配了十七名宪兵。17 日松井石根骑马举行入城式，19 日起日军焚烧了全城约三分之一。
埋尸团体统计超过 15.5 万具遗体。东京审判认定被杀害者 20 万以上，南京审判认定 30 万以上。松井 1948 年被处以绞刑。城墙还在。
三集完结，完整长片下周上线。
#南京大屠杀 #南京保卫战 #抗日战争 #历史 #国家公祭
```

## 8. 注意

- **年龄限制：**这一集讲大屠杀，但画面上没有尸体，YouTube 一般不会加年龄限制。如果被加了，可以按「教育 / 纪实」类内容申诉。
- **埋尸数字：**15.5 万出自东京审判判决书的原话，其中崇善堂的记录有争议，片中卡片已经注明「one record disputed」。
- **14,777：**这是日方自己清点的俘虏数（幕府山一带），片中已经注明「the Japanese army's own count」。
- **中文 hashtag：**国内平台可以加 #国家公祭，12 月 13 日是国家公祭日，发布时间离这天很近。
