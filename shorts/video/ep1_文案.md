## 3. 简介（英文，直接粘贴）

```
December 1937. Up to half a million people inside a walled city, an army behind them, and a river with no bridge.

Nanjing had been China's capital for ten years. After three months of fighting in Shanghai, 160,000 Japanese troops chased the retreat up the Yangtze. Most of China's generals wanted to give the city up and save the army. Chiang Kai-shek refused, and handed Nanjing to the one general who said it could be held: Tang Shengzhi. Tang walled up the gates and sent the ferries away, so his men could not run. On 8 December the last outer line broke. By the next morning about 70,000 Japanese soldiers were at the wall on three sides. On the fourth side was the river.

Part 1 of 3. Part 2 tomorrow: four days at the wall.
```
